CAMP BASE

"Where you're far far...far away from society... be prepared to learn wilderness skills such as making fire's, make friends, learn responsibility through our correction courses and of course have "fun" by joining manditory events such as color war. We have it all so theres no need for you to escape or end this experience early. Listen to your counselors...stay on base and things will be fine. Enjoy your stay with us this summer camper and again...dont try to escape."

"Alright campers since we are half way through summer we are going to head into town for the night where there's a lot to eat and hang out at the arcade to reward you all being on your best behavior ." This is perfect you think. The counselors will transport you from the canoes to the town and then you can make your big break for it. This camp sucked , it had little to no activities and always felt eerie, the counselor's were so ominous about everything they had off putting smiles to go with their flat tone all the time and when other campers misbehaved they would return back in the same strange demeanor as well. You weren't about to turn into a robot over some "bad grades", half the people here were already brainwashed you just had to pretend , so you didn't get chosen next.
